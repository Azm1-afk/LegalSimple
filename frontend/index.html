Important legal information

LegalSimple is intended to support document understanding and preliminary review. Its outputs should not be treated as definitive legal advice. Users should consult a qualified legal professional when dealing with serious disputes, deadlines, court matters, or decisions with significant legal consequences.

Legal FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of documents can LegalSimple review?

LegalSimple provides reading guidance for common contracts, agreements, notices, letters, and other text-based legal PDF documents.

Does LegalSimple replace a lawyer?

No. It is designed to make information easier to understand and assist with preliminary document review. Professional legal advice may still be necessary.

Can it compare two versions of a contract?

The comparison guide organizes common change categories and explains why changed wording may deserve closer attention.

What topics will the Legal FAQ cover?

The FAQ includes plain-language guidance about LegalSimple, traffic matters, renting, and labor and employment questions.
