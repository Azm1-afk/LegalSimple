Document 2 Updated wording pattern

Payment is due within 14 days after an invoice is issued.

How to explain the change

A shorter payment period gives the paying party less time to respond after an invoice is issued.

Important Changes Requiring Attention

Review pattern · notice period

Shorter termination notice period

Modified
Document 1 Earlier wording pattern

Either party may end the agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.

Document 2 Updated wording pattern

Either party may end the agreement by giving 14 days' written notice.

Plain-language explanation
A shorter period allows less time to prepare before the agreement ends.
Why it may matter
This type of change may deserve additional attention because it could affect planning time.
