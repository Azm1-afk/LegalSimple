Risk reference

Common clause risk indicators

Review guide

Risk categories pair clear labels with short explanations to help prioritize a manual review. Apply them only after reading the complete clause and its surrounding terms.

High risk

Rights or liability language

Terms that may significantly limit options or increase exposure can require close review.

Medium risk

Cost or timing terms

Obligations involving fees, renewal dates, or notice periods may need careful checking.

Low risk

Routine contract language

Routine provisions may require less immediate attention but should still be read in context.

Risk labels can support review prioritization; they do not determine whether a clause is legal, fair, or suitable for a specific situation.
